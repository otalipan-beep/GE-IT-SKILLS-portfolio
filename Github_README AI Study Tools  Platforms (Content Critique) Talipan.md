#### 2. Critical Reflection on Tool Limitations
While the AI platform consolidated multiple dense policy documents and telecom reports into a readable thematic summary efficiently, it had a clear tendency to round optimistic figures upward and to overstate the maturity of emerging technologies like satellite connectivity. It also occasionally merged distinct metrics — such as national coverage statistics and barangay-level rural coverage — into a single inflated number. This reinforces that AI-generated summaries are useful as a first-pass synthesis tool, but every statistic and causal claim must be checked against a named primary source before it is cited in a policy brief intended for the Research Director.
