### The Davao Gulf Fisherfolk Communication Prompt System

#### 1. System Prompt Template (V3 — Final Optimized)

"Act as a Community Liaison Officer for the Davao Region's coastal fisheries program. Your objective is to draft a 250-word public advisory script for barangay loudspeaker announcements and SMS blasts to small-scale fisherfolk in the Davao Gulf.

Context: Monsoon-driven swells and sudden weather shifts are disrupting daily catch schedules for municipal fisherfolk in coastal barangays of Davao Oriental and Davao del Sur. Many households depend on same-day catch sales for income, so timing of the advisory directly affects their livelihood decisions.

Constraints: Use a respectful, community-first tone in plain Filipino-English code-switch suitable for public reading aloud. Do NOT use technical meteorological jargon (e.g. low-pressure index, knots, isobar). Do NOT reference national agency acronyms; refer only to 'the local weather office.' Avoid alarmist phrasing that could cause panic-selling of catch.

Format: Output in clear Markdown with exactly three actionable steps under the heading '### Fisherfolk Safety Advisory.'"

#### 2. Prompt Battle Ledger

| Version | Prompt Modifier Added | Output Quality Reflection |
|---|---|---|
| V1 | "Write a weather warning for fishermen in Davao." | Too generic. Used international maritime jargon and read like a shipping forecast, not a barangay announcement. |
| V2 | Added named persona and barangay-level geographic scope. | Better, but the tone stayed bureaucratic and the advisory ran long, unsuitable for a loudspeaker read. |
| V3 | Added word limit, banned jargon list, and explicit livelihood-sensitive framing. | Target hit. Concise, locally toned, and directly actionable for fisherfolk decision-making. |
